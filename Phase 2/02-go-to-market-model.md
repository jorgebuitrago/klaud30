# Klaud Revamp Strategy  
## Phase 2 – Go-To-Market Model (Revised)

With **Phase 0** and **Phase 1** completed, Phase 2 defines **how Klaud will reach the market, expand revenue, and reposition itself commercially** in a way that is realistic for a 2-person expert firm.

This revised phase incorporates two strategic constraints:

1. **Broadcom remains a core account**, not just a legacy revenue source. Klaud must actively communicate its new identity to Broadcom and use its existing VMware/Broadcom specializations, certifications, and services capabilities to increase trust, improve positioning, and grow weekly allocation within the current relationship.
2. **Project Recovery is a protected pillar reserved for Broadcom-related work.** Klaud’s external partner motion and future partner engine will focus on only four pillars:
   - VMware Platform Architecture
   - VCF Modernization
   - Advanced Networking
   - Kubernetes / Tanzu Enablement

This is the correct adaptation to the current market. Broadcom’s revised partner program explicitly rewards services capabilities, certifications, and technical expertise, including VMware-focused specializations and Broadcom Knights with a VMware Cloud Foundation specialty. Broadcom has also stated that partner-led services are central to customer success and that customers should be matched with partners able to unlock the full value of VCF. ([news.broadcom.com](https://news.broadcom.com/partners/redesigned-broadcom-partner-program-delivers-more-value-to-partners-and-customers))

---

# 1. Phase 2 Objective

Phase 2 has a dual objective:

## A. Defend and expand the Broadcom relationship
Klaud must stop behaving like a passive overflow supplier and instead present itself to Broadcom as a **specialized services partner for advanced VMware platform work**.

## B. Build a partner-first growth motion outside Broadcom
Klaud must create a repeatable model to win work through other partners without building a traditional sales organization.

This means Phase 2 is not only a market-access plan. It is also a **commercial repositioning plan**.

---

# 2. Core Strategy: Two Go-To-Market Tracks

Klaud will operate through two parallel tracks.

## Track A – Strategic Account Expansion Inside Broadcom

Broadcom is no longer just a source of legacy work. It is Klaud’s most important **anchor account** and should be managed intentionally.

The goal is to evolve Broadcom’s internal perception of Klaud from:

- overflow delivery resource  
to
- **trusted specialist partner for advanced VMware engagements**

This is especially relevant because Broadcom’s partner model now places more value on technical certifications, services capabilities, and post-sales delivery enablement. Broadcom also highlights early services involvement as a way to improve customer outcomes and increase solution adoption. ([news.broadcom.com](https://news.broadcom.com/partners/redesigned-broadcom-partner-program-delivers-more-value-to-partners-and-customers))

## Track B – External Partner-Led Growth

Outside Broadcom, Klaud will pursue a focused partner strategy built around four specialized pillars:

- VMware Platform Architecture
- VCF Modernization
- Advanced Networking
- Kubernetes / Tanzu Enablement

These are aligned with Broadcom’s current VCF direction, which emphasizes standardized private cloud, integrated automation, and Kubernetes capabilities inside the VCF stack. Broadcom’s own VMware materials now position VCF, VCF Automation, VCF Networking, and vSphere Kubernetes Service as core components of the platform direction. ([blogs.vmware.com](https://blogs.vmware.com/cloud-foundation/2025/07/24/unlocking-new-possibilities-extend-vmware-cloud-foundation-with-enhanced-vsphere-kubernetes-service-add-ons/))

# 3. Track A – Broadcom Re-Identity and Account Expansion

## 3.1 Strategic Goal

Increase Broadcom-sourced revenue quality, stability, and weekly utilization by repositioning Klaud as:

**A high-trust specialist services partner for complex VMware platform outcomes**

The aim is not to ask Broadcom for “more work” in the abstract.  
The aim is to help Broadcom understand **what kind of work Klaud should own**.

---

## 3.2 What Klaud Must Communicate to Broadcom

Klaud’s message to Broadcom should be:

- Klaud is not a generic VMware subcontractor
- Klaud is strongest in **advanced, non-standard, high-risk VMware scenarios**
- Klaud can be used earlier in projects to reduce delivery risk
- Klaud’s certifications, specializations, and partner enablement status validate this positioning
- Klaud can complement larger delivery partners by handling the most technically specialized portions of customer engagements

This is consistent with Broadcom’s own messaging: the company has emphasized that partner expertise should be brought in earlier, that services matter more as products become more complex, and that highly trained partners should help customers achieve fuller VCF adoption. ([news.broadcom.com](https://news.broadcom.com/partners/broadcom-expands-popular-broadcom-knights-program-to-support-vcf-customers))

---

## 3.3 Broadcom-Facing Positioning Statement

Klaud should communicate a concise identity such as:

> Klaud is a specialized VMware services partner focused on complex VMware platform architecture, VCF modernization, advanced networking, and Kubernetes enablement for customer environments that fall outside standard deployment patterns.

And for internal Broadcom stakeholders:

> Klaud is most valuable when a customer needs senior specialist expertise beyond standard VCF implementation, especially where architecture ambiguity, platform complexity, networking depth, automation, or Kubernetes enablement increase delivery risk.

## 3.7 Broadcom Engagement Model

Broadcom-facing work should be deliberately split into two groups.

### Group 1 – Strategic Specialization Work
Preferred work:
- VCF modernization
- advanced networking
- Kubernetes/Tanzu or VKS enablement
- automation-related architecture
- non-standard platform design

### Group 2 – Protected Recovery Work
Reserved only for the Broadcom relationship:
- stalled projects
- failed rollouts
- unstable platform deployments
- architecture rework under deadline pressure

This keeps the recovery pillar where it already has trust and context, without making external growth dependent on emergency consulting.

# 4. Track B – External Partner-Led Growth

## 4.1 Strategic Goal

Build new revenue through partner-led delivery, but only around the four proactive pillars:

- VMware Platform Architecture
- VCF Modernization
- Advanced Networking
- Kubernetes / Tanzu Enablement

This is the external growth engine.

Project Recovery is deliberately excluded from the external message, because it is harder to scale commercially and is better used as a trusted escalation capability for Broadcom.

---

## 4.2 External Market Position

Externally, Klaud should position itself as:

**A specialist VMware platform consultancy for advanced VCF, networking, automation, and Kubernetes outcomes in LATAM and the Caribbean**

This positioning works because Broadcom’s ecosystem is increasingly concentrated around partners that can deliver the full value of VCF, and Broadcom has narrowed its partner ecosystem toward those with deeper expertise and stronger service capability. ([news.broadcom.com](https://news.broadcom.com/partners/broadcom-vmware-cloud-foundation-strategy-for-service-providers))

---

## 4.3 External Target Partners

Klaud should prioritize three partner profiles.

### A. Regional VMware-focused integrators
These partners often have customer access but limited depth in:
- advanced VCF
- networking
- automation
- Kubernetes enablement

### B. Larger multinational integrators needing specialist subcontractors
These firms often hold the contract but lack deep product specialists in-region.

### C. MSPs or private cloud providers evolving toward higher-value VMware services
Especially those trying to strengthen:
- private cloud architecture
- standardization
- Kubernetes services
- advanced network design

---

## 4.4 External Partner Value Proposition

Klaud should offer partners a simple message:

> We help you win and deliver the VMware work that is too specialized, too architecture-heavy, or too non-standard for a generalist delivery team.

This message should be supported by four external value themes:

### Specialist depth
Klaud goes deeper than generalist cloud teams.

### Faster technical confidence
Klaud reduces risk in design and execution for non-standard projects.

### White-label friendliness
Klaud can work discreetly as part of the partner’s delivery model.

### Regional fit
Klaud brings bilingual capability and regional familiarity across LATAM and the Caribbean.

# 6. Revised Partner Engagement Models

The external partner model should use only three engagement patterns.

## Model A – Architecture Specialist
Klaud enters during design or pre-delivery phases to define architecture and reduce project risk.

Aligned pillars:
- VMware Platform Architecture
- VCF Modernization
- Advanced Networking
- Kubernetes / Tanzu Enablement

## Model B – Specialist Delivery Lead
Klaud leads the specialized technical components of a broader project.

Aligned pillars:
- VCF Modernization
- Advanced Networking
- Kubernetes / Tanzu Enablement

## Model C – Embedded Expert Residency
Klaud participates as a high-seniority resident specialist in complex initiatives where scope evolves over time.

Allowed examples:
- VCF automation programs
- Tanzu/VKS platform build-out
- NSX segmentation or federation programs
- private cloud engineering

Not allowed:
- generic staff augmentation
- anonymous “extra hands” roles
- operational support substitution

# 7. Messaging Split: Broadcom vs External Market

A key refinement in this revised phase is that Klaud should not use the same message everywhere.

## Broadcom-facing message
“Use Klaud for advanced VMware specialization and recovery of high-risk VMware customer engagements.”

## External partner-facing message
“Use Klaud for advanced VMware platform architecture, VCF modernization, advanced networking, and Kubernetes enablement.”

This split is important because Broadcom already knows Klaud in delivery and recovery contexts. External partners should meet Klaud first through **proactive, strategic, specialization-led services**, not emergency rescue work.
