# SEO-Oriented URL Structure

Keep URLs short, direct, and technical.

## Recommended URLs
- /
- /services
- /when-to-use-klaud
- /partner-collaboration
- /about
- /contact
- /privacy-policy
- /terms

## Optional URLs later
- /reference-scenarios
- /certifications

Avoid overly clever URLs, marketing-style slugs, or deep nested structures.


# Redirect Strategy

If you are relaunching the site, redirects matter a lot.

## Redirect principles

Every old page that has any backlinks, traffic, or brand history should redirect to the closest new equivalent page.

## Recommended redirect mapping logic

### Old general company pages
- old home variants → /
- old services pages → /services
- old consulting / solutions pages → /services
- old partner-related pages → /partner-collaboration
- old case study / project pages → /about or /reference-scenarios
- old contact pages → /contact

## Important note

Do not redirect everything to the Home page.

That weakens relevance and is not ideal for SEO or user experience.

Use page-to-page relevance redirects wherever possible.

# What Not to Include at Launch

To keep the site sharp, I would avoid the following in phase one:

- blog with frequent-post expectations
- news section
- huge resource library
- many sub-service pages
- jargon-heavy product pages
- generic marketing pages full of fluff
- separate pages for every technology unless SEO demand later proves it necessary

For Klaud, too many pages would weaken the positioning.

The site should feel like a specialist firm that knows exactly what it is.

# My Strategic Recommendation

For Klaud, the strongest launch structure is a 6-page core site built around one powerful landing page and a few high-value supporting pages.

That gives you:

- clarity for visitors
- strong positioning for partners
- good SEO foundations
- low maintenance overhead
- a structure that can grow later without needing to be rebuilt

The key is not more pages.

The key is that every page answers a specific business question with precision.
