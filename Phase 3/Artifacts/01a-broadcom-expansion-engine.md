# Klaud Revamp Strategy  
## Phase 3A – Broadcom Account Expansion Engine

Phase 3A defines how Klaud will **systematically expand its position inside Broadcom** during 2026.

This phase is based on three realities:

1. Broadcom has shifted VMware into a **partner-led services model**, where partner capabilities, certifications, and measurable customer outcomes matter more than passive subcontracting. ([news.broadcom.com](https://news.broadcom.com/partners/driving-success-together-broadcoms-partner-mission?utm_source=chatgpt.com))
2. Broadcom is concentrating customer value around **VMware Cloud Foundation**, and it increasingly highlights partners that can architect, deploy, and drive adoption of VCF and adjacent capabilities such as automation, networking, and Kubernetes. ([news.broadcom.com](https://news.broadcom.com/partners/driving-success-together-broadcoms-partner-mission?utm_source=chatgpt.com))
3. Broadcom’s partner ecosystem is becoming more focused and value-based, which creates an opening for a small specialist like Klaud to be recognized not for scale, but for **high-impact expertise in difficult customer scenarios**. ([news.broadcom.com](https://news.broadcom.com/partners/broadcom-advantage-partner-program-roadmap-success?utm_source=chatgpt.com))

The purpose of this phase is therefore not simply to “ask for more work.”  
It is to make Broadcom see Klaud as a **specialist partner category** inside its delivery ecosystem.

---

# 1. Strategic Objective

The objective of Phase 3A is to increase Klaud’s share of Broadcom-sourced work by improving four things:

- **Visibility** inside Broadcom
- **Trust** in Klaud’s specialist capabilities
- **Early-stage involvement** in the lifecycle of customer engagements
- **Weekly allocation quality and volume**

By the end of this phase, Broadcom should no longer think of Klaud primarily as:

- overflow delivery capacity
- generic VMware consulting support
- emergency-only escalation

Instead, Broadcom should think of Klaud as:

**A specialist services partner for advanced VMware platform architecture, VCF modernization, advanced networking, Kubernetes enablement, and protected recovery of high-risk engagements.**

---

# 2. Phase 3A Design Principles

## 2.1 Broadcom is an account, not a channel

Klaud must manage Broadcom as a **strategic named account**.

That means Broadcom should have:

- defined stakeholders
- a message map
- service positioning
- account assets
- review cadence
- explicit growth goals

This is a major shift from the old model, where Broadcom was treated as a source of assigned work.

---

## 2.2 Klaud must sell a category, not availability

Broadcom should not hear:

- “We have bandwidth”
- “We can help if needed”
- “We’re available for projects”

Broadcom should hear:

- “Use Klaud when the VCF deployment is non-standard”
- “Use Klaud when networking is multi-site or architecture-heavy”
- “Use Klaud when Kubernetes enablement exceeds generalist depth”
- “Use Klaud when automation is central to platform success”
- “Use Klaud when a Broadcom-led project is technically at risk”

This aligns with Broadcom’s own emphasis on partner services capability, technical specialization, and lifecycle value creation around VCF. ([news.broadcom.com](https://news.broadcom.com/partners/driving-success-together-broadcoms-partner-mission?utm_source=chatgpt.com))

---

## 2.3 Recovery stays protected, not promoted externally

Project Recovery remains a **Broadcom-only protected capability**.

This means:

- it continues to support the existing trusted relationship
- it remains part of Klaud’s Broadcom value
- it is not used as the core message for external partner growth

That split is strategically sound because Broadcom already knows Klaud in high-risk delivery contexts, while the broader market should meet Klaud through proactive specialization.

---

# 3. Broadcom Account Hypothesis

Phase 3A is built on the following account hypothesis:

> Broadcom is more likely to increase Klaud’s weekly allocation and trust if Klaud becomes easier to classify internally.

Today, Klaud risks being perceived as “good technical people we know.”

That is not enough.

Broadcom needs to be able to classify Klaud quickly as:

- **specialists in advanced VMware platform design**
- **trusted experts for VCF modernization**
- **go-to niche experts for advanced NSX / multi-site networking**
- **capable Kubernetes / Tanzu enablement specialists**
- **safe recovery option for Broadcom-owned at-risk projects**

The easier Klaud is to classify, the easier it becomes for internal Broadcom stakeholders to route work to Klaud.

# 4. Stakeholder Map Inside Broadcom

Klaud should build a lightweight stakeholder map for Broadcom with four groups.

## 4.1 Delivery Controllers

These are the people who influence who gets staffed and when.

Typical examples:

- services coordinators
- resource managers
- delivery leads
- engagement managers

### What they care about
- can Klaud be trusted
- where Klaud fits
- how quickly Klaud can help
- whether Klaud reduces delivery risk

### What Klaud must communicate
- where Klaud is strongest
- what projects are ideal
- why early involvement improves outcomes

---

## 4.2 Technical Influencers

These are the architects and senior engineers who shape delivery decisions.

Typical examples:

- solution architects
- principal consultants
- lead specialists
- VCF or platform architects

### What they care about
- technical depth
- architecture judgment
- credibility in complex scenarios
- ability to work in non-standard environments

### What Klaud must communicate
- specialization areas
- advanced product depth
- examples of solved complex scenarios
- ability to complement larger teams

Broadcom’s own partner content increasingly highlights highly trained technical experts, including Broadcom Knights and VCF-specialized partner talent, as differentiators for customer success. ([news.broadcom.com](https://news.broadcom.com/partners/broadcom-expands-popular-broadcom-knights-program-to-support-vcf-customers?utm_source=chatgpt.com))

---

## 4.3 Commercial / Account Stakeholders

These are the people who influence how services are used in broader customer motions.

Typical examples:

- account teams
- partner-facing technical contacts
- regional customer success stakeholders
- pre-sales leadership connected to services delivery

### What they care about
- customer confidence
- faster adoption
- lower delivery risk
- stronger renewals / customer outcomes

### What Klaud must communicate
- Klaud helps customers unlock more value from VCF
- Klaud improves success in technically difficult engagements
- Klaud helps Broadcom match the right expertise to the right project

This is closely aligned with Broadcom’s public framing that services drive adoption, that partner expertise should help customers realize the full value of VCF, and that partner impact should be measurable across the lifecycle. ([news.broadcom.com](https://news.broadcom.com/partners/driving-success-together-broadcoms-partner-mission?utm_source=chatgpt.com))

---

## 4.4 Executive Sponsors / Senior Champions

These are the few people whose informal support can materially change Klaud’s visibility.

### What they care about
- delivery quality
- customer outcomes
- risk containment
- trusted specialist partners

### What Klaud must communicate
- Klaud solves difficult problems that generalist teams struggle with
- Klaud is low-overhead and high-trust
- Klaud should be engaged earlier, not only after problems appear

# 5. Klaud’s Broadcom-Facing Capability Map

Broadcom should see Klaud through a simple capability map.

## 5.1 Primary Specialist Capabilities

### VMware Platform Architecture
Use Klaud when:
- customer architecture is unclear
- the environment is heterogeneous
- standard blueprints do not fit
- implementation risk is high without senior design authority

### VCF Modernization
Use Klaud when:
- the customer is moving toward a VCF operating model
- standardization is needed
- lifecycle and automation questions are central
- the deployment is more complex than a basic rollout

Broadcom continues to center partner value on leading with VCF and driving adoption through services. ([news.broadcom.com](https://news.broadcom.com/partners/driving-success-together-broadcoms-partner-mission?utm_source=chatgpt.com))

### Advanced Networking
Use Klaud when:
- NSX architecture is non-trivial
- the environment is multi-site
- federation, segmentation, GSLB, or complex traffic design is involved

### Kubernetes / Tanzu Enablement
Use Klaud when:
- Kubernetes must be enabled within the VMware estate
- Tanzu or vSphere Kubernetes capabilities are part of the target platform
- the customer needs platform integration, not just container theory

Broadcom’s current VCF messaging explicitly ties VCF to automation, networking, and Kubernetes capabilities as part of the platform’s value proposition. ([news.broadcom.com](https://news.broadcom.com/partners/broadcom-vmware-cloud-foundation-strategy-for-service-providers?utm_source=chatgpt.com))

## 5.2 Protected Broadcom-Only Capability

### Recovery of High-Risk Broadcom-Owned Engagements
Use Klaud when:
- a Broadcom-led deployment is stalled
- architecture assumptions have failed
- technical execution is at risk under deadline pressure
- specialized remediation is needed quickly

This remains a Broadcom-only pillar.

# 6. Broadcom Positioning Architecture

Phase 3A should organize Klaud’s message into three layers.

## 6.1 Master Positioning Statement

> Klaud is a specialist VMware services partner for advanced platform architecture, VCF modernization, advanced networking, Kubernetes enablement, and recovery of technically at-risk Broadcom customer engagements.

## 6.2 Internal Routing Statement

> Use Klaud when a VMware customer engagement is non-standard, architecture-heavy, advanced in networking or Kubernetes scope, automation-sensitive, or technically at risk.

## 6.3 Outcome Statement

> Klaud reduces delivery risk, accelerates architecture clarity, and increases the probability of customer success in advanced VMware platform initiatives.

# 8. Operating Motions for Account Expansion

Phase 3A should not rely on a single conversation or presentation.  
It should use repeated operating motions.

## 8.1 Motion 1 – Re-Identity Announcement

Objective:
Formally communicate that Klaud has evolved into a clearly specialized services partner.

Actions:
- send updated one-pager to key Broadcom stakeholders
- request short account re-introduction meetings
- present focus areas and ideal routing scenarios
- highlight certifications and specialist status

Output:
Broadcom stakeholders understand Klaud’s new category.

---

## 8.2 Motion 2 – Internal Education

Objective:
Teach Broadcom when Klaud should be brought in earlier.

Actions:
- hold short technical capability briefings
- walk through “when to use Klaud” scenarios
- share anonymized case examples
- reinforce the four proactive pillars plus protected recovery

Output:
Klaud becomes easier to route internally.

---

## 8.3 Motion 3 – Early Lifecycle Capture

Objective:
Move upstream in the engagement lifecycle.

Actions:
- ask to be considered during discovery or design for complex VCF, networking, or Kubernetes opportunities
- position Klaud as a risk-reduction specialist
- offer architecture authority before implementation starts

This is strategically aligned with Broadcom’s stated direction that services should drive adoption and customer outcomes, not just post-sale execution. ([news.broadcom.com](https://news.broadcom.com/partners/driving-success-together-broadcoms-partner-mission?utm_source=chatgpt.com))

Output:
Klaud is engaged before projects become rescue missions.

---

## 8.4 Motion 4 – Specialist Routing

Objective:
Create pattern recognition inside Broadcom.

Actions:
- repeatedly attach Klaud to the same categories
- avoid generic availability language
- reinforce a narrow specialist identity

Output:
Broadcom begins to associate Klaud with specific advanced use cases.

---

## 8.5 Motion 5 – Recovery Reputation Preservation

Objective:
Protect the existing trust Klaud already has in difficult projects.

Actions:
- continue taking the right high-risk recovery work from Broadcom
- document wins
- convert recovery wins into proof of specialist authority

Output:
The old relationship remains productive while the new one becomes more strategic.

# 10. Metrics for Phase 3A

Phase 3A should be measured with a small set of practical metrics.

## 10.1 Visibility Metrics
- number of Broadcom stakeholders briefed
- number of internal champions identified
- number of asset pack shares or follow-up requests

## 10.2 Positioning Metrics
- number of opportunities tied to one of the four proactive pillars
- number of times Klaud is invited before implementation starts
- number of Broadcom requests that reference Klaud’s specialist areas

## 10.3 Allocation Metrics
- average weekly hours
- number of active Broadcom engagements
- share of Broadcom work that is specialist vs generic

## 10.4 Quality Metrics
- number of successful recoveries
- number of repeat requests from the same Broadcom stakeholders
- number of projects where Klaud influences architecture, not only execution

# 11. Risks and Failure Modes

## Risk 1 – Klaud stays positioned as generic overflow
### Mitigation
Use strict specialist language and repeat it consistently.

## Risk 2 – Broadcom only remembers Klaud for rescue work
### Mitigation
Push early-stage architecture and modernization conversations.

## Risk 3 – Certifications are treated as decoration
### Mitigation
Tie each certification or specialization to a routing scenario.

## Risk 4 – Klaud gets pulled into operational work
### Mitigation
Reinforce boundaries and preferred engagement types early.

## Risk 5 – Too many Broadcom contacts, no real champion
### Mitigation
Identify 2–3 high-value internal supporters and deepen those relationships first.
