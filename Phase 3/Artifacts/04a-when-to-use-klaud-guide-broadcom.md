# When to Use Klaud  
## Broadcom Expansion Engine Guide (Path 3A)

This document helps Broadcom teams identify situations where engaging Klaud accelerates customer success, removes technical blockers, and enables expansion opportunities.

Klaud is not a traditional system integrator. It operates as a specialized architecture and delivery partner focused on advanced VMware Cloud Foundation environments, complex networking architectures, and Kubernetes enablement on VMware platforms.

Broadcom teams should consider engaging Klaud when deep technical expertise is required to unblock architecture decisions, support complex implementations, or stabilize challenging environments.

---

# Role of Klaud in the Broadcom Ecosystem

Klaud acts as an architecture-focused specialist partner that supports Broadcom initiatives in the following ways:

• Accelerating complex VMware Cloud Foundation deployments  
• Supporting advanced NSX architectures and networking designs  
• Enabling Kubernetes platforms on VMware infrastructure  
• Stabilizing difficult or stalled implementations  
• Assisting partners and customers with complex architectural decisions  

Klaud typically works alongside system integrators, MSPs, or internal Broadcom teams, providing deep technical expertise rather than replacing delivery partners.

---

# Situations Where Klaud Should Be Engaged

## 1. Complex VMware Cloud Foundation Deployments

Engage Klaud when a customer environment requires architecture design or implementation support for VMware Cloud Foundation.

Typical scenarios include:

• Multi-cluster VCF deployments  
• Complex networking requirements within VCF environments  
• Private cloud modernization initiatives  
• Migration from legacy vSphere architectures to VCF  
• Automation-driven infrastructure environments  

Klaud can assist with architecture planning, deployment design, and technical validation.

---

## 2. Advanced NSX Networking Architectures

Klaud should be considered when networking requirements go beyond standard NSX implementations.

Examples include:

• Multi-site NSX architectures  
• NSX Federation environments  
• Microsegmentation strategies across large environments  
• Integration with external networking infrastructure  
• Troubleshooting complex networking behavior  

These engagements often involve architectural design, validation, and troubleshooting.

---

## 3. Kubernetes Platforms on VMware

When customers are adopting Kubernetes platforms on VMware infrastructure, Klaud can assist with architecture and enablement.

Typical situations include:

• Tanzu platform architecture design  
• Kubernetes cluster strategy across VMware environments  
• Integration of Kubernetes with existing enterprise infrastructure  
• Platform enablement for development teams  

Klaud supports architecture design and technical enablement rather than application development.

---

## 4. Architecture Advisory for Strategic Opportunities

Broadcom teams may engage Klaud during strategic customer engagements that require deep technical architecture expertise.

Examples include:

• Architecture workshops with enterprise customers  
• Pre-deployment design validation  
• Technical risk assessment for large deployments  
• Design reviews before production rollout  

Klaud provides architecture-focused guidance that helps de-risk large initiatives.

---

## 5. Rescue or Stabilization of Challenging Deployments

Klaud can be engaged when VMware environments are experiencing technical challenges that require specialized expertise.

Examples include:

• Stalled VMware Cloud Foundation deployments  
• NSX environments with architectural issues  
• Kubernetes platform instability on VMware infrastructure  
• Complex upgrade or migration challenges  

These engagements typically focus on stabilization, remediation, and architecture correction.

---

# How Klaud Typically Engages

Klaud works through several engagement patterns depending on the situation.

Common collaboration models include:

• Architecture advisory during planning phases  
• Specialist technical delivery within partner-led projects  
• Embedded architecture expertise for complex initiatives  
• Short-term technical intervention to unblock projects  

Klaud is comfortable working under partner leadership, within Broadcom-led initiatives, or in white-label collaboration models.

---

# What Klaud Does Not Typically Provide

To ensure proper routing of opportunities, it is useful to understand where Klaud is not typically engaged.

Klaud is generally not used for:

• Large-scale general system integration projects  
• Long-term operational managed services  
• Application development  
• Basic VMware deployments that do not require advanced architecture expertise  

Those engagements are typically better served by integrators or MSP partners.

---

# How to Initiate a Klaud Engagement

Broadcom teams who believe Klaud may be a good fit for an opportunity can initiate engagement through the following steps:

1. Identify the architectural challenge or technical requirement.  
2. Determine whether specialized expertise is required.  
3. Engage Klaud for an architecture discussion or discovery call.  
4. Define the scope of the engagement in collaboration with the delivery partner (if applicable).

Early involvement often helps prevent architecture issues later in the project lifecycle.

---

# Summary

Klaud is best engaged when advanced technical architecture expertise is required to enable VMware Cloud Foundation adoption, support complex networking architectures, or guide Kubernetes enablement on VMware infrastructure.

By collaborating with Broadcom teams and delivery partners, Klaud helps accelerate deployments, reduce technical risk, and support successful customer outcomes.
