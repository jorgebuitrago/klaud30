# When to Use Klaud  
## External Partner Collaboration Guide (Path 3B)

This guide helps system integrators, MSPs, and delivery partners identify when engaging Klaud can strengthen project delivery, reduce technical risk, and support complex VMware-based environments.

Klaud operates as a specialized architecture and technical delivery partner focused on advanced VMware Cloud Foundation environments, NSX networking architectures, and Kubernetes enablement on VMware platforms.

Rather than competing with integrators or managed service providers, Klaud typically works alongside partner teams, providing specialized expertise that complements existing delivery capabilities.

---

# Klaud’s Role in Partner-Led Projects

Klaud supports delivery partners by contributing deep technical expertise in situations where advanced architecture design, troubleshooting, or platform enablement is required.

Typical collaboration models include:

• Specialist architecture advisory during planning phases  
• Embedded technical expertise within partner delivery teams  
• Short-term technical intervention for complex environments  
• White-label support under the partner’s leadership  

This approach allows partners to expand their delivery capabilities while maintaining full control of the customer relationship.

---

# Situations Where Partners Should Consider Klaud

## 1. VMware Cloud Foundation Architecture and Deployment

Klaud can support partners delivering VMware Cloud Foundation environments that require specialized architecture expertise.

Typical situations include:

• Initial architecture design for VCF environments  
• Multi-cluster or large-scale VCF deployments  
• Private cloud modernization initiatives  
• Migration from legacy vSphere environments into VCF  
• Design validation before production rollout  

Klaud can assist with architecture planning, deployment strategy, and technical validation.

---

## 2. Advanced NSX Networking Architectures

NSX deployments often involve complex networking requirements that extend beyond standard configurations.

Klaud can assist when projects involve:

• Multi-site NSX architectures  
• NSX Federation designs  
• Large-scale microsegmentation strategies  
• Integration with existing enterprise networking environments  
• Troubleshooting complex networking behavior  

These engagements typically focus on architecture design, validation, and technical troubleshooting.

---

## 3. Kubernetes Enablement on VMware Platforms

When partners are enabling Kubernetes platforms on VMware infrastructure, Klaud can provide architectural guidance and platform design support.

Common scenarios include:

• Tanzu platform architecture design  
• Kubernetes cluster strategy within VMware environments  
• Integration of Kubernetes with enterprise infrastructure  
• Platform enablement for development teams  

Klaud focuses on platform architecture and enablement rather than application development.

---

## 4. Complex Architecture Design or Technical Risk

Partners may engage Klaud when projects involve significant architectural complexity or technical risk.

Examples include:

• Large enterprise environments with multiple infrastructure domains  
• Multi-site infrastructure architectures  
• Integration across virtualization, networking, and container platforms  
• Projects where design validation is critical before implementation  

In these situations, Klaud helps ensure the architecture is stable, scalable, and aligned with VMware best practices.

---

## 5. Stabilizing Challenging or Stalled Projects

Klaud can assist partners when a project encounters unexpected technical challenges.

Typical scenarios include:

• VMware Cloud Foundation deployments that are not progressing as expected  
• NSX networking issues affecting environment stability  
• Kubernetes platform challenges within VMware environments  
• Complex upgrades or migrations that require advanced troubleshooting  

These engagements are typically short-term interventions focused on stabilization and remediation.

---

# Collaboration Models

Klaud is flexible in how it collaborates with delivery partners.

Common engagement approaches include:

• Architecture advisory during project planning  
• Specialist delivery within partner-led projects  
• Embedded technical expert within partner teams  
• White-label collaboration where Klaud operates behind the partner brand  

This flexibility allows partners to expand their capabilities without disrupting existing delivery models.

---

# What Klaud Does Not Typically Provide

To ensure the right fit, it is useful to understand where Klaud is generally not engaged.

Klaud is typically not used for:

• Large-scale system integration delivery without architectural complexity  
• Long-term managed services or operations  
• Application development projects  
• Basic VMware deployments that do not require specialized expertise  

These types of engagements are usually better handled directly by integrators or MSP providers.

---

# How to Engage Klaud

Partners who believe Klaud may be able to support a project can initiate collaboration through a simple process.

1. Identify the technical challenge or architecture requirement.  
2. Determine whether specialized VMware architecture expertise is required.  
3. Initiate a technical discussion with Klaud to review the opportunity.  
4. Define the scope and engagement model that best supports the project.

Early collaboration helps reduce technical risk and improves project outcomes.

# Summary

Klaud supports delivery partners by providing specialized architecture expertise for complex VMware Cloud Foundation environments, advanced NSX networking architectures, and Kubernetes platforms on VMware infrastructure.

By collaborating with partners rather than replacing them, Klaud helps strengthen delivery teams, accelerate projects, and ensure successful outcomes for customers.
