# Klaud – Specialist VMware Platform Consulting

## Overview

Klaud is a specialist consulting firm focused on **advanced VMware platform architecture and complex infrastructure environments**.

We support **VMware partners, system integrators, MSPs, and enterprise customers** across **Central America, South America, and the Caribbean**, helping deliver VMware projects that require **deep technical expertise beyond standard deployments**.

Klaud is typically engaged when VMware environments involve:

- non-standard VMware Cloud Foundation architectures
- advanced NSX networking designs
- Kubernetes enablement within VMware platforms
- automation-driven private cloud initiatives
- heterogeneous infrastructure environments

Our role is to provide **senior technical authority, architecture clarity, and specialized implementation expertise** for projects where complexity exceeds the capabilities of generalist delivery teams.

# Core Specialization Areas

## VMware Platform Architecture

Architecture design and advisory for complex VMware environments.

Typical scenarios include:

- heterogeneous VMware infrastructure environments
- early architecture design for private cloud initiatives
- integration of multiple VMware platform components
- architecture validation for high-risk projects

---

## VMware Cloud Foundation Modernization

Transformation and modernization of VMware environments toward **VMware Cloud Foundation (VCF)** platforms.

Typical engagements include:

- VCF architecture design
- migration from legacy vSphere environments
- platform standardization initiatives
- lifecycle and operational alignment for VCF environments

---

## Advanced Networking Architectures

Design and implementation of complex VMware networking environments using **NSX**.

Areas of expertise include:

- multi-site architecture design
- NSX Federation
- Global Server Load Balancing (GSLB)
- segmentation and microsegmentation architectures
- advanced traffic routing strategies

---

## Kubernetes / Tanzu Enablement

Integration of **Kubernetes platforms within VMware infrastructure environments**.

Typical scenarios include:

- VMware Tanzu platform design
- Kubernetes architecture strategy
- integration between Kubernetes clusters and VMware infrastructure
- platform enablement for development teams
