# Klaud Revamp Strategy
## Phase 1 – Service Portfolio Definition

With Klaud’s identity defined in Phase 0, the next step is to translate that identity into **clear, repeatable service offerings**.

The goal of this phase is to transform Klaud’s expertise into **productized consulting services** that partners and customers can easily understand, scope, and procure.

A well-defined service portfolio provides several advantages:

- Reduces sales friction for partners
- Clarifies the outcomes Klaud delivers
- Makes subcontracting easier
- Enables repeatable delivery
- Avoids “hourly consulting” engagements

Each service defined below includes:

- **Service description**
- **Typical scenarios**
- **Scope of work**
- **Expected duration**
- **Inputs required**
- **Outputs delivered**
- **Target customer profile**

---

# Service Pillar 1 – VMware Platform Architecture & Design

## Description

This service provides **architecture definition and design authority** for organizations implementing or modernizing VMware platforms.

It is particularly valuable in environments where:

- standard deployment patterns do not apply
- infrastructure is heterogeneous
- advanced platform capabilities are required
- internal teams lack experience with newer VMware technologies

This engagement establishes a **production-ready architecture that can be implemented safely and operated effectively**.

---

## Typical Scenarios

- Organizations planning **VMware Cloud Foundation deployments**
- Customers modernizing **legacy vSphere environments**
- Environments integrating **NSX, vSAN, and automation platforms**
- Projects requiring **multi-site architectures**
- Early stages of **Tanzu platform adoption**

---

## Scope of Work

Typical activities include:

- Current infrastructure assessment
- Architecture requirements analysis
- Platform topology design
- Integration design (networking, storage, identity)
- Operational model definition
- Lifecycle and upgrade planning
- Documentation of architecture decisions

---

## Expected Duration

Typically:

**2–4 weeks**

Depending on environment size and complexity.

---

## Inputs Required

Customer provides:

- Access to infrastructure documentation
- Access to architecture diagrams
- Stakeholder interviews
- Platform requirements and constraints

---

## Outputs Delivered

- Complete architecture design documentation
- Platform topology diagrams
- Operational model recommendations
- Implementation roadmap
- Risk analysis and mitigation plan

---

## Target Customers

- Mid-market enterprises with VMware environments
- Service providers deploying private cloud platforms
- Partners requiring architecture expertise for complex projects

---

## Description

This service helps organizations adopt or modernize **VMware Cloud Foundation environments** and align them with modern infrastructure practices.

The focus is not simply deploying VCF, but ensuring that the platform is **operationally sustainable and aligned with enterprise infrastructure standards**.

---

## Typical Scenarios

- Organizations migrating from **traditional vSphere architectures to VCF**
- Environments with **partially deployed VCF platforms**
- Customers needing **standardization across clusters and domains**
- Platforms requiring **automation enablement**

---

## Scope of Work

Typical activities include:

- VCF environment assessment
- Domain architecture review
- Standardization of clusters and resource domains
- Lifecycle management planning
- Operational readiness improvements
- Integration with existing enterprise services

---

## Expected Duration

Typically:

**3–6 weeks**

Depending on platform maturity and complexity.

---

## Inputs Required

Customer provides:

- VCF environment access
- Infrastructure documentation
- Platform configuration exports
- Stakeholder interviews

---

## Outputs Delivered

- VCF modernization roadmap
- Platform standardization plan
- Lifecycle and patching strategy
- Automation opportunity identification

---

## Target Customers

- Enterprises adopting VMware private cloud architectures
- Organizations modernizing legacy VMware platforms
- Service providers standardizing infrastructure offerings

# Service Pillar 5 – Project Recovery & Platform Stabilization

## Description

This service addresses **stalled or failing VMware infrastructure initiatives**.

Klaud is frequently engaged when:

- projects have missed deadlines
- deployments are incomplete or unstable
- architectural assumptions have proven incorrect
- internal teams lack expertise in specific VMware technologies

The goal is to **stabilize the platform and guide the project toward successful completion**.

---

## Typical Scenarios

- Failed upgrades or migrations
- Incomplete VCF deployments
- Broken NSX networking environments
- Tanzu deployments that cannot proceed
- Platform instability impacting project timelines

---

## Scope of Work

Typical activities include:

- Technical diagnosis
- Architecture review
- Root cause identification
- Platform stabilization
- Remediation planning
- Technical leadership for project completion

---

## Expected Duration

Typically:

**1–3 weeks for diagnosis**  
Additional time depending on remediation scope.

---

## Inputs Required

Customer provides:

- Infrastructure access
- platform logs and configuration
- architecture documentation
- stakeholder interviews

---

## Outputs Delivered

- root cause analysis report
- remediation roadmap
- architecture corrections
- stabilization plan

---

## Target Customers

- Organizations with failing VMware initiatives
- Partners needing expert intervention
- Customers facing project deadlines
